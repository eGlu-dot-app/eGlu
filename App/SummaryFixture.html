FIELD NOTES · BETTER BROWSING

A quieter way to browse

A good browser gives your attention back. Keep related pages in spaces, find what matters, and carry on without losing your place.

Make room for the work

Group tabs by the project they belong to. A space keeps its own pages and sign-ins, so you can return to the work exactly where you left it.

Read first, decide next

A short summary can help you decide what deserves a closer read. The original page stays open behind it, ready when you are.

Small, deliberate tools make the web feel less crowded. Keep the page at the centre and let everything else get out of the way.
